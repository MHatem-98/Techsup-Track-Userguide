Exporting Reports

Download your reports in PDF or Excel format for record-keeping, sharing with your employer, or for personal documentation.

Export Steps

1

Generate Your Report

View the report you want to export (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly)

2

Click Export

Look for the "Export" button, usually at the top-right of the report

3

Choose Format

Select either PDF or Excel (.xlsx) format

4

Download

The file will automatically download to your device

File Naming: Reports are automatically named with the report type and date (e.g., "Attendance_Report_Jan_2024.pdf")
