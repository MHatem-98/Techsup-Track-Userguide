User Guide

Master Techsup Track

Your complete guide to using Techsup Track for flexible work management, attendance tracking, and direct communication with your management.

Mobile App Features

Everything you need is in the palm of your hand.

Key Mobile Features

  • One-tap check-in and check-out
  • Real-time working hours counter
  • View daily and weekly reports instantly
  • Receive instant notifications
  • Send and receive messages with management
  • Upload files on the go
  • GPS verification for accurate location tracking
  • Offline mode (basic features available without internet)
  • Fingerprint authentication for quick login
  • Battery optimization for extended use

Getting Started with the App

1

Download and Install

Search "Techsup Track" in Google Play or App Store and tap Install

2

Open the App

Tap the app icon to launch Techsup Track

3

Login

Enter your email and password to access your account

4

Enable Permissions

Allow location, notification, and camera permissions for full functionality

5

Start Using

You're ready! Check in, view reports, and communicate with your manager right from your phone

Security Tip: Enable biometric authentication (fingerprint) in your app settings for secure and quick access.
