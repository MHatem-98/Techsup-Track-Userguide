Your complete guide to using Techsup Track for flexible work management, attendance tracking, and direct communication with your management.

Daily Reports

View detailed information about each workday.

Information Included:

  • Check-in and check-out times
  • Total working hours
  • Break duration
  • Attendance status (Present, Absent, Leave)
  • Location history (GPS coordinates)
  • Any notes or remarks from management

How to Access:

1

Go to Reports

Click the "Reports" section in your dashboard menu

2

Select Daily Report

Choose "Daily Report" from the options

3

Pick a Date

Use the date picker to select which day you want to view

4

View Details

The report displays all your attendance information for that day
