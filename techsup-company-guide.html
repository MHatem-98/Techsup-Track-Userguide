Getting Started with Techsup Track

Welcome to Techsup Track's Company Administration Portal! This comprehensive guide will help you maximize the platform to manage your workforce, monitor attendance, and ensure compliance with Saudi labor regulations.

What is Techsup Track for Companies?

Techsup Track is an enterprise attendance tracking and workforce management platform certified by the Saudi Ministry of Human Resources. It enables you to manage flexible work arrangements, monitor employee attendance in real-time, generate compliance reports, and maintain effective communication with your team.

Key Features for Your Business

  • Custom control panel for independent company management
  • Organization structure setup (departments, teams, hierarchies)
  • Role-based access control and permissions management
  • Real-time employee attendance and GPS tracking
  • Comprehensive daily, weekly, and monthly reporting
  • Data filtering by project, employee, or task
  • Internal messaging system for team communication
  • Document and file sharing capabilities
  • Full compliance with Saudi remote work regulations
  • Integration with HR and payroll systems
Important: As a company administrator, you have significant responsibilities. Ensure you understand Saudi labor law requirements and this platform's compliance features.

Company Registration Process

Follow these steps to set up your company account on Techsup Track.

1

Visit the Registration Page

2

Use your Chamber of Commerce registration number

Create the primary administrator account with email and strong password. This person will have full access to company settings.

4

Enter company address, phone number, website, and primary contact person details.

5

Upload Required Documents

Provide certified copies of:

  • Chamber of Commerce certificate
  • Tax registration certificate
  • Company authorization letter
6

Review and accept the Service Agreement. This includes compliance with Saudi labor regulations and Techsup Track policies.

7

Verification & Activation

Your company will be verified by Techsup Support within 24-48 hours. You'll receive an activation email when your account is ready.

Roles & Permissions Management

Default Roles

Role Description Permissions
Company Admin Full system access All features, settings, user management, reports
Department Manager Department-level control View department employees, attendance, reports; manage leaves
Team Lead Team supervision View team members, attendance, basic reports

Configuring Work Locations

Set up the physical locations or coordinates where employees can check in. This is critical for GPS-based attendance verification.

Why Work Locations Matter: Techsup Track uses GPS verification to ensure employees are checking in from authorized work locations. This prevents fraudulent attendance records and ensures compliance.

Labor Law Compliance

Ensure your company meets all Saudi Arabian labor law requirements using Techsup Track.

Key Compliance Areas

Working Hours Limits

Saudi law limits standard workday to 8 hours. Techsup Track automatically flags violations and calculates overtime correctly.

Break Requirements

Employees are entitled to breaks. The system ensures break deductions are accurate and compliant.

Leave Entitlements

Annual leave, sick leave, and other entitlements are tracked and managed according to Saudi labor law.

Overtime Management

Hours beyond standard are flagged as overtime and tracked separately for compensation.

Remote Work Compliance

Data Security & Privacy

Techsup Track implements enterprise-level security to protect sensitive employee data.

Security Features

  • End-to-end encryption of all data transmission
  • Secure password hashing and two-factor authentication
  • Role-based access control (employees see only their data)
  • Audit logs tracking all system access and changes
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • GDPR and Saudi data protection compliance
  • Data backup and disaster recovery systems
  • Secure data deletion upon contract termination

Best Practices for Admins

1. Use Strong Passwords: Create complex admin passwords. Change them regularly and never share.
2. Enable Two-Factor Auth: Always enable 2FA on your admin account for extra security.
3. Regular Access Audits: Periodically review who has access and remove unused accounts.
4. Limit Admin Users: Only give admin access to people who absolutely need it.
5. Secure Export Handling: When exporting reports with employee data, store and transmit securely.
