Getting Started with Techsup Track

Welcome to Techsup Track! This guide will walk you through every step of using our platform. Whether you're a new user or looking to explore advanced features, you'll find everything you need here.

What is Techsup Track?

Techsup Track is a flexible work and attendance tracking platform certified by the Saudi Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development. It allows you to record your attendance, view comprehensive reports, and communicate directly with your management—all from one secure platform.

Key Features for You

  • One-click attendance and departure recording
  • Automatic calculation of daily working hours
  • Comprehensive daily, weekly, and monthly reports
  • Direct communication channel with your management
  • File sharing and document upload capabilities
  • Mobile app access (Android & iOS)
  • Full compliance with Saudi remote work requirements

Registration Process

Follow these steps to create your Techsup Track account as an employee or job seeker.

1

Visit the Registration Page

Navigate to the Employee Login page on Techsup Track's website and click "Create your account".

2

Enter Your Email Address

Provide a valid email address. This will be your primary login credential and where we send important notifications.

Use an email you check regularly
3

Create a Strong Password

Set a secure password with at least 8 characters, including uppercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

Example: MySecure@Pass123
4

Provide Personal Information

Fill in your full name, phone number, and national ID. Ensure all details match your official documents.

5

Agree to Terms & Conditions

Read and accept the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. This ensures compliance with Saudi labor regulations.

6

Verify Your Email

Check your email inbox for a verification link. Click it to confirm your account. If you don't see it, check your spam folder.

7

Account Activation

Your account will be activated by your employer after they approve your registration. You'll receive a confirmation email once your account is ready to use.

Attendance System

The core feature of Techsup Track is its flexible and accurate attendance tracking system. Learn how to properly record your work time.

How It Works: The system automatically calculates your working hours, lunch breaks, and rest periods. All data is timestamped and GPS-verified for accuracy and compliance with Saudi labor regulations.

Attendance Status Options

You are at work and actively working. Record your check-in when you start your workday.

You're taking a lunch break or rest period. The system automatically tracks break duration and deducts it from your working hours.

You did not show up for work or requested leave. Contact your management if this wasn't planned.

You requested approved leave (vacation, sick leave, etc.). This is recorded separately from absences.

Check-In Process

Record your arrival at the start of your workday with a single click.

1

Open Techsup Track

Log in to your dashboard or open the mobile app

2

Locate the Check-In Button

On your dashboard, you'll see a prominent "Check In" button. It's usually displayed in the main widget.

3

Click Check-In

Click the button. The system will automatically capture:

  • Current time (timestamp)
  • Your location (GPS)
  • Device information
4

Confirmation

You'll receive an on-screen confirmation: "Check-in successful at [Time]"

5

Begin Work

Your status is now "Present" and your working time counter has started. Get to work!

Important: Always check in from your designated work location. GPS verification is required for compliance.

Finish Your Work

Complete all tasks and ensure you're ready to finish your workday

2

Open Techsup Track

Go to your dashboard or mobile app

3

Click Check-Out

The "Check Out" button will be visible. Click it to record your departure time.

4

System Calculation

The system will automatically:

  • Calculate total working hours
  • Deduct any break time
  • Update your daily report
5

Confirmation

Receive confirmation: "Check-out successful. You worked [X hours]"

Daily Summary: After check-out, your dashboard updates with today's working hours summary

Time Tracking & Breaks

The system automatically tracks all your time, including breaks and lunch periods.

How Breaks Work

The system recognizes when you're on break and automatically pauses your work timer. You can manually mark a break or the system can detect inactivity. Break time is NOT counted toward your working hours—only your actual work time is recorded.

Recording a Break

1

Click "Take Break"

When you're taking a break, click the break button on your dashboard

2

Pause Timer

Your working time counter pauses automatically

3

Resume Work

Click "Resume" when your break is over to continue tracking

Most companies allow 1 hour for lunch break. Check your employment contract for your specific break policy.

Direct Communication with Management

Techsup Track includes a built-in messaging system for seamless communication between you and your management.

Why Use Messaging?

  • Submit questions or concerns to your manager
  • Receive important announcements and task assignments
  • Request time off or leave approvals
  • Share documents or reports for review
  • Get immediate responses without leaving the platform
  • Maintain a complete record of all communications

How to Send a Message

1

Navigate to Messages

Click the "Messages" tab in your dashboard

2

Start New Conversation

Click "New Message" or select a recipient from your contacts

3

Type Your Message

Write your message in the text field. You can format text with bold, italic, and other options.

4

Send

Click "Send" to deliver your message instantly

Response Times: Managers typically respond within 24 business hours. For urgent matters, contact your manager directly by phone.

File Sharing & Uploads

Upload documents, files, and supporting materials directly within your messages.

Supported File Types

PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), Images (.jpg, .png, .gif), and other common document formats up to 10MB per file

Upload Steps

1

Open Message Compose

Click in the message field or create a new message

2

Click Attachment Icon

Look for the paperclip or attachment icon next to the send button

3

Select File

Browse your device and choose the file you want to upload

4

Confirm Upload

The file will preview before sending. Click send to attach it to your message
