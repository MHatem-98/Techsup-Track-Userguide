Master Techsup Track

Your complete guide to using Techsup Track for flexible work management, attendance tracking, and direct communication with your management.

Registration Process

Follow these steps to create your Techsup Track account as an employee or job seeker.

1

Visit the Registration Page

Navigate to the Employee Login page on Techsup Track's website and click "Create your account".

2

Enter Your Email Address

Provide a valid email address. This will be your primary login credential and where we send important notifications.

Use an email you check regularly
3

Create a Strong Password

Set a secure password with at least 8 characters, including uppercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

Example: MySecure@Pass123
4

Provide Personal Information

Fill in your full name, phone number, and national ID. Ensure all details match your official documents.

5

Agree to Terms & Conditions

Read and accept the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. This ensures compliance with Saudi labor regulations.

6

Verify Your Email

Check your email inbox for a verification link. Click it to confirm your account. If you don't see it, check your spam folder.

7

Account Activation

Your account will be activated by your employer after they approve your registration. You'll receive a confirmation email once your account is ready to use.
