Master Techsup Track

Your complete guide to using Techsup Track for flexible work management, attendance tracking, and direct communication with your management.

Login & Dashboard Overview

Logging Into Your Account

1

Go to Login Page

Visit techsuptrack.com and click "Employee Login"

2

Enter Credentials

Input your registered email and password

3

Two-Factor Authentication (Optional)

If enabled, enter the code sent to your phone or email

4

Access Your Dashboard

You're now in! Your personalized dashboard displays all your information at a glance.

Dashboard Components

Welcome Section: Displays your name, current status (online/offline), and quick action buttons
Attendance Widget: Shows today's check-in/check-out status and current working time
Quick Stats: Weekly working hours summary, absences, and performance overview
Notifications: Messages from management and system alerts
