Performance Analytics & Insights

Performance Analytics & Insights

Use data-driven insights to make better decisions about your workforce.

Key Metrics

Attendance Rate

Percentage of days employees were present. High rates indicate good workforce stability.

Punctuality Score

Percentage of on-time arrivals. Identifies patterns of late check-ins.

Average Working Hours

Track if employees are meeting required hours or working overtime patterns.

Absence Trends

Identify patterns: frequent absences on specific days, particular employees with issues.

Leave Patterns

Analyze approved leave usage and plan for coverage.

Using Analytics for Decision-Making

  • Identify underperforming employees who need support
  • Plan staffing for busy periods
  • Spot potential policy violations or attendance issues
  • Benchmark against company standards
  • Make data-backed hiring decisions
