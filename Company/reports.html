Generating & Viewing Reports

Generating & Viewing Reports

Access comprehensive reports to monitor attendance, performance, and ensure compliance with regulations.

Types of Reports Available

Daily Attendance Report

Shows all check-ins, check-outs, absences, and working hours for a specific day

Weekly Attendance Report

Weekly summary showing total hours, punctuality, absences, and trends

Monthly Report

Complete monthly attendance data for payroll, compliance, and analysis

Performance Analytics

Charts and graphs showing attendance trends, punctuality rates, and team productivity

Leave & Absence Report

Detailed breakdown of approved leaves, unexcused absences, and pending requests

Compliance Report

Government compliance documentation meeting Saudi labor law requirements

Accessing Reports

1

Navigate to Reports

Click "Reports" from the main menu

2

Select Report Type

Choose which report you need (daily, weekly, monthly, etc.)

3

Set Parameters

Select date range, department/team, or specific employees to include

4

Generate Report

Click "Generate" to create the report

5

View & Analyze

Review the report on screen, or proceed to export it
