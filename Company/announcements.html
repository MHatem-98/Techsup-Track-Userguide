Company Announcements

Company Announcements

Broadcast important messages to all employees or specific groups.

Creating Announcements

1

Navigate to Announcements

Go to "Communications" → "Announcements"

2

Create New Announcement

Click "Create Announcement"

3

Write Title & Content

Create a clear title and detailed announcement content

4

Select Audience

Choose who sees it: all employees, specific departments, or certain roles

5

Schedule (Optional)

Publish immediately or schedule for a specific date/time

6

Publish

Click "Publish" and all relevant employees are notified

Best Practices: Use announcements for company-wide updates, policy changes, holidays, or important deadlines. Use direct messages for individual feedback.
