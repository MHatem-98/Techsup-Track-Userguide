Create System Email

Send a system-generated email to one or more companies directly from the Super Admin panel.

Sending a System Email

The Create Email page lets Super Admins broadcast a system email to selected companies — for example, a platform announcement, policy update, or maintenance notice.

Selecting Recipients

1

Open Create Email

From the sidebar, go to Create System Email.

2

Review the Companies IDs List

Every company on the platform is listed with its name and registered email address in parentheses.

3

Choose Recipients

Check the box next to each company you want to email, or click Select All in the top-right to include every company at once.

4

Continue to Compose

After selecting the companies, continue through the form to write the email subject and message content, then send it.

Companies IDs Field is Required

The field is marked with a red asterisk (*) — you must select at least one company before you can proceed.

Click Back in the top-right at any time to leave the form without sending an email.

Warning: Using Select All sends the email to every company's registered email address on the platform — double-check your recipient list before continuing for anything other than a platform-wide announcement.
