Trashed Emails

Manage deleted emails — restore accidentally deleted messages or permanently purge them from the system.

Managing Deleted Emails

The Trashed Emails page displays all emails that have been deleted or removed. From here, you can restore emails you accidentally deleted or permanently remove them if they're no longer needed. This provides communication recovery protection and audit trail control.

Understanding Trashed Emails

When an email is deleted, it doesn't disappear immediately. Instead, it moves to the trash where it remains recoverable for a specified grace period. This safety measure protects against accidental deletions and ensures you have time to decide on permanent removal while maintaining your communication history.

Trashed Email Summary Cards

Deleted Emails

Total count of all deleted emails in trash.

Deletion Date

When emails were deleted (most recent first).

Recovery Window

Days remaining before permanent purge.

Restoreable

Emails available for recovery action.

Searching and Filtering Trashed Emails

1

Search by Subject or Content

Type keywords to find specific deleted emails by subject line or message content.

2

Sort by Deletion Date

Sort the list by when emails were deleted to locate recently deleted messages.

3

Filter by Participant

Narrow results by sender or recipient name.

4

Filter by Date Range

Specify deletion date range to view emails deleted within a specific period.

Trashed Emails Table

Column What it shows
Subject Email subject line and message ID
From / To Sender and primary recipients
Deleted At When the email was deleted (timestamp)
Days Until Purge How many days remain before permanent deletion
Participants All people involved in the email thread
Actions Restore or permanently delete options

Restoring an Email

To bring a deleted email back to your inbox:

1

Find the Email

Locate the deleted email in the trash using search filters or date sorting.

2

Click Restore

Select the Restore action to move the email back to your inbox.

3

Verify Restoration

The email is now restored and accessible in your Emails list with all metadata intact.

Permanently Deleting an Email

To completely remove an email from the system:

  1. ⚠️Select the Delete Permanently action on the email row.
  2. ⚠️Confirm the action when prompted — this cannot be undone.
  3. ⚠️The email is immediately and irreversibly removed from the system.
Warning: Permanent deletion is irreversible. Emails cannot be recovered after permanent removal. Archive or export important email threads before permanently deleting them.
Tip: Regularly review and empty your trash to conserve storage. Archive important email communications before deletion if you need to maintain long-term compliance or audit records.
