SUPER ADMIN GUIDE

Archive Companies

Review companies that have been removed from the active list, and restore or permanently delete them.

Working with Archived Companies

When a company is removed from the active Companies List, it isn't deleted permanently right away — it moves to the Archive Companies view first, giving you a safety net before permanent removal.

Finding an Archived Company

1

Open Archive Companies

From the Companies List, click Archive Companies, or use the sidebar link directly.

2

Search

Use the Search box to find a company by name, email, website, or country.

3

Sort the List

Use Sort By (e.g. Deleted At) and Sort Direction (Ascending/Descending) to organize the archive, then click Search to apply — or Clear Filters to reset.

Reading the Archive Table

Column What it shows
Logo The company's logo, if one was set
Company Information Company name, internal ID, and website
Contact Information The company's registered email and phone number
Location Country or city the company was registered under
Deleted At Date and time the company was archived
Actions Open the Actions menu to restore the company back to the active list, or remove it permanently

Bulk Actions

Clear All Archive Companies

Permanently removes every company currently in the archive. This action cannot be undone.

Back to Active Companies

Returns you to the main Companies List.

Warning: "Clear All Archive Companies" and individual permanent-delete actions cannot be reversed. Restore any company you still need before clearing the archive.
