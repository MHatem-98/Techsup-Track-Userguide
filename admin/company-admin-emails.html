Emails

Manage company emails — track sent messages, monitor read status, and organize email communications across departments.

Managing Company Emails

The Emails page is your centralized email management hub. Track outgoing emails, monitor delivery and read status, organize messages by department and location, and maintain an audit trail of all company communications.

Email Summary Overview

Total Emails

Complete count of all emails sent and received.

Sent Emails

Emails successfully sent by your company.

Read Emails

Messages that recipients have opened and read.

With Attachments

Emails that include attached files.

Filtering and Searching Emails

1

Search by Subject or Content

Type keywords to find emails by subject line or content.

2

Filter by Status

Show emails by read status — All Status, Read Only, Received Only.

3

Filter by Department & Location

Narrow results by sending department or recipient location.

4

Filter by Date Range

Specify From and To dates to view emails within a specific timeframe.

Emails Table Columns

Column What it shows
Subject Email subject line and thread ID
Participants Senders and recipients involved
Company Which company sent or received the email
Read Status Whether recipients have opened and read the message
Attachments Number of files attached, if any
Date When the email was sent (timestamp)
Actions View, download, or delete options

Quick Actions

  • + Compose Email — draft and send a new email
  • View Trashed — recover deleted emails
Tip: Use email templates for recurring communications. Always include clear subject lines and verify recipients before sending important company emails.
