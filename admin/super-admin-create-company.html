SUPER ADMIN GUIDE

Create New Company

Onboard a new company onto Techsup Track by filling in its information, location, and reporting settings.

Creating a Company

From the Companies List, click + Add Company (or Create New Company in the sidebar) to open the creation form.

Step 1 — Company Information

1

Company Name & Email

Company Name* and Company Email* are required fields — the email will be used for account and system communications.

2

Website & Phone Number

Company Website* is required. Phone Number is optional but recommended for contact purposes.

3

Company Address

Enter the company's full street address.

4

Labor Office ID & Sequence Number

Enter the company's Labor Office ID and Sequence Number for compliance and internal record-keeping (if applicable).

5

Company Logo

Click Choose File to upload the company logo.

The logo file must be smaller than 2MB.

Step 2 — Location

1

Country

Select the company's country from the dropdown (defaults to Saudi Arabia).

2

State/Province & City

Enter the state or province, and the city where the company is based.

3

ZIP/Postal Code & Timezone

Enter the postal code and select the correct Timezone — this affects how attendance timestamps are calculated for this company.

Step 3 — Settings

1

Currency Name

Set the currency used for this company's subscription and payroll figures (defaults to Saudi Riyal).

2

Date Format

Choose how dates are displayed throughout this company's account.

3

Status

Set the initial status of the company — typically Active so it can start being used right away.

Step 4 — Reports

Toggle which automated reports this company will receive:

  • Daily Reports — a summary sent every day
  • Weekly Reports — a summary sent every week
  • Monthly Reports — a summary sent every month
  • Attendance Tracking — enables GPS-verified check-in/check-out tracking for this company

Step 5 — Save

Once all required fields are filled in, click Create to finish onboarding the company, or Cancel to discard the form and return to the Companies List.

Tip: Double-check the Timezone and Currency settings before saving — these are harder to change accurately once the company starts logging attendance and contract data.
