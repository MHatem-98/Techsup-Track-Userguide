SUPER ADMIN GUIDE

Companies List

View, search, and manage every company registered on Techsup Track from one central list.

Managing Companies

The Companies page is where you review the status of every company on the platform, spot missing managers or admins, and jump into any company's record.

Companies Summary Cards

Total / Active / Inactive / Deleted Companies

Four cards at the top give you the total company count and a breakdown by status, including how many have been deleted (archived).

Companies Without Managers / Admins

These cards flag companies missing a manager, an admin, or both — useful for spotting accounts that need follow-up.

Contracts Status Strip

Just below the summary cards, a status strip shows the total number of contracts across all companies broken into Active, Draft, Terminated, Pending, Expired, and No Contract.

Searching & Filtering the Company List

1

Search

Type a company name, website, city, or country into the Search box.

2

Filter by Status

Use the Status dropdown to show only Active, Inactive, or all companies.

3

Filter by Admin or Contract Status

The Admins Status and Contract Status dropdowns let you isolate companies missing an admin, or with a specific contract state.

4

Sort the Results

Choose a field under Sort By (e.g. Company Name) and pick Sort Direction — Ascending or Descending.

5

Apply or Clear Filters

Click Search to apply your filters, or Clear Filters to reset the list to its default view.

Reading the Companies Table

Column What it shows
Logo The company's uploaded logo, if one has been set
Company Information Company name, internal ID, and website link
Contact Information The company's registered email and phone number
Location City/region the company is registered in
Status Active or Inactive badge
Current Subscription The company's current subscription fee (e.g. 25 SR)
Contract Information Contract start/end date range and current contract status
Actions Open the Actions menu to view, edit, or manage that company

Use the Show [X] entries per page control to change how many rows display, and the page numbers at the bottom to move through the full list.

Adding & Archiving Companies

Add Company

Click + Add Company in the top-right of the Companies List to open the company creation form. See Create New Company for the full walkthrough.

Archive Companies

Click Archive Companies to view companies that have been removed from the active list. See Archive Companies for details.

Tip: Use the "Companies without Admins and Managers" card as a quick to-do list — these accounts likely need onboarding follow-up before they can be used effectively.
