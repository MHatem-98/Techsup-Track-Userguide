Holidays Management

Manage employee holidays, vacation days, and time-off requests

Overview

The Holidays Management page is your central hub for managing all employee time-off requests including vacation days, sick leave, personal days, and other holiday types. You can view holiday statistics, search for specific holidays, filter by various criteria, and create new holiday records.

Understanding Holiday Statistics

The top of the page displays four key statistics:

1

Total Holidays

Complete count of all holiday records in your system, including past, present, and future holidays.

2

Public Holidays

Shows the number of public holidays that apply to all employees. These are typically national or regional holidays when the whole company is closed.

3

Private Holidays

Displays the count of private/personal holidays — time off specific to individual employees for personal reasons.

4

Annual Holidays

Shows the count of annual vacation days that employees take as part of their yearly entitlements.

Search and Filter Options

Use these tools to find specific holidays:

1

Search Box

Enter a holiday name, employee name, or other keywords to quickly locate specific holiday records.

2

Type Filter

Filter holidays by type: All Types, Public, Private, Annual, Sick Leave, Personal Days, etc.

3

Status Filter

Filter by status: All, Approved, Pending, Rejected, or Cancelled.

4

Date Range Filter

Select From Date and To Date to filter holidays within a specific time period.

5

Filter & Reset Buttons

Click Filter to apply your criteria. Click Reset to clear all filters and see all holidays.

Holidays List Table

The holidays list displays records with the following columns:

•

Holiday Name

The name or title of the holiday (e.g., "Summer Vacation", "Sick Leave", "Public Holiday").

•

Type

The category of holiday (Public, Private, Annual, Sick, etc.).

•

Employee

The name of the employee assigned to this holiday (if applicable).

•

Status

Current status of the holiday request (Approved, Pending, Rejected, etc.).

•

Start Date & End Date

The date range for the holiday period.

•

Duration

The number of days included in this holiday period.

•

Actions

Options to view details, edit, approve/reject, or delete the holiday record.

Creating a New Holiday

To add a new holiday to the system:

  1. 1Click the + Add New Holiday button in the top-right corner.
  2. 2Select the holiday type (Public, Private, Annual, Sick Leave, etc.).
  3. 3Fill in the holiday details (name, dates, employee assignment, reason).
  4. 4Click Save to create the holiday record.
Tip: For detailed instructions on adding new holidays, refer to the "Add New Holiday" guide page.

Best Practices for Holiday Management

  • Plan Ahead: Create holiday records in advance so employees know what time off is available.
  • Approve Timely: Review and approve or reject holiday requests promptly to avoid confusion.
  • Maintain Records: Keep accurate records of all holidays for payroll and compliance purposes.
  • Track Balances: Monitor individual employee holiday balances to ensure fair distribution.
  • Use Descriptions: Add notes or reasons when creating holiday records for future reference.
Tip: Use the search and filter options frequently to quickly find specific holidays or track holidays by employee, type, or time period.
