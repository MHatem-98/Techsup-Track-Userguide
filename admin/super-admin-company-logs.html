Company Logs

A running audit trail of every system-level event that happens to a company record — who changed what, and when.

Understanding Company Logs

The Logs page under Companies gives Super Admins a chronological audit trail of company-level events — for example, a company being set to active, deactivated, or otherwise updated by the system or an admin.

Reading a Log Entry

Column What it shows
Timestamp The exact date and time the event occurred
Company Name Which company the event relates to
Severity Badge An INFO badge marks a standard, non-critical system event
Event Type A short system code describing what happened (for example, a company being set to active)
Performed By The admin or system account responsible for the change
View Click View on any row to see full details of that log entry

Why It Matters

  • Trace exactly when a company's status changed and who made the change
  • Investigate unexpected activity on a company account
  • Provide an audit trail for compliance or support requests
Tip: Logs are listed newest-first, so the most recent activity for any company always appears at the top of the list.
